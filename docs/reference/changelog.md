## Change policy

Policy, schema, command, or deadline changes require a source-pin update and review. Editorial clarification that does not change behavior may ship without a version bump but must retain last-updated metadata.

Deprecated advice stays documented for one handbook version when it may affect existing tasks, with a replacement link and removal date.
